I've been doing a few illustrations on commission and for collab projects throughout August, and I'm going to be drawing something for another collab soon. In the middle of all this, I really wanted to draw something just for myself. I like doing comms and collabs, but sometimes a man needs a change of pace.

I recently rediscovered the artist 108号 (of the doujin circle Slice Slime), who drew an absolutely amazing (and very NSFW) cover to their Infinite Stratos doujin *How To Train Your Rabbit.* I first saw the cover a long time ago, and I think it's the reason I drew [Bunny Cecilia 1](/art/bunny-cecilia) in 2023.

I decided to draw Cecilia again, partly to see how I've improved and partly to try out some techniques I don't normally do. Instead of going from sketch to lineart to color and rendering, I did the sketch, then color plus really rough light and shadow, *then* the lineart. The idea was to see if it would make me draw thinner lineart, maybe with less hatching, which I normally like to do. It worked, kinda, but the effect is not huge.

It *did* make me try doing colored lineart again. I had this phase in late 2024 and early 2025 where I tried to do pretty clean lines and colored lineart (e.g. [Green Travels](/art/green-travels)), and I decided that it wasn't really working for me. But this time, when I'm doing lineart on top of color, I start noticing that the lineart clashes with the nice colors I put down. To remedy that, I turned alpha lock on for the lineart layer and started re-coloring it.

Recently, I've been playing around in Cyberpunk 2077's Photo Mode, and I only really discovered how fun it can be when the game makes you take a picture with the Us Cracks in Kerry Eurodyne's questline. There's all these camera settings and you can set up lights of different colors and stuff. The light positioning UX is a little clunky, and I wish my computer could run the game smoother, but it's actually a pretty cool way to experiment with photography without actually leaving your home.

I mention this mainly because it made me try some bolder lighting, with this big spotlight on Cecilia like I did in a few glamor shots in Photo Mode. I might play around with more lighting in the game and see if I can apply that in my illustrations.
