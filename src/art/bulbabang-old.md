This was the first design concept for my Bulbabang illustration, which I abandoned because it was too time-consuming. You can compare it with the [final illustration](/art/bulbabang), which retains a lot of the same elements from Viridian Beedrill's story, but in a completely different structure.

I'm a little sad that I couldn't fit in the newspaper, Le Monde Pokémon ("The Pokémon World"), into the final one. It's a parody of the French newspaper, [Le Monde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Monde), with a headline inspired by this [TV news segment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HXstRfE9AVU) about Cyberdépendance ("cyber-addiction") that became a meme for calling MMORPGs, *meuporgs*.
