I recently received a request for an emblazonment by [EtnadRehsif](https://rollofarms.miraheze.org/wiki/GreiiN:576), who has been commissioning various artists to emblazon their personal arms. Thank you very much for the commission!

Since Etnad's coat of arms has this nice space theme going on, I decided to give the mullets of 6 (the 6-pointed stars) a little bit of a glow to them. It would be interesting to see that on an actual, real-life shield, maybe with glow-in-the-dark paint or some kind of LED backlighting.

The crab reminds me of one of those crab pen holders which are quite charming.

## Blazon
### Escutcheon
Sable semy of mullets of 6 Argent a manta ray bendwise Or.

### Crest
A demi crab Gules holding in its claws a mullet of six Argent.
