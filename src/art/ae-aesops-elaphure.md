Many months ago I was commissioned (again) to make a series of Discord server icons. This is the same client who previously commissioned such gems as [Enraptured Athoracophoridae](/art/enraptured-athoracophoridae) and [Erudite Australopithecus](/art/erudite-australopithecus), so I knew I was going to be in for something interesting. Just like last time, I was given a list of phrases whose words started with E and A, then asked to illustrate them. I was given minimal direction other than the occasional explanation when I asked what some of the words meant.

An elaphure, also known as Père David's deer, is a nearly extinct deer species native to the subtropical river valleys of China. An Aesop is one of many moralistic short stories that usually involves two animals.

I think this is my second favorite drawing out of the commission set. It's inspired by the [Take On Me MV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djV11Xbc914&list=RDdjV11Xbc914), with the sketch of the motorcycle dude breaking out of the girl's drawing into the real world. I tried out the Krita watercolor brushes, and I think it makes the elaphure look really good. Strong storybook vibes.

By the way, the Snail and the Elaphure is not a real Aesop, nor did I write an actual story for this.
