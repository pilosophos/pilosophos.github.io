Some folks in the heraldry community on Reddit were doing tarot cards inspired by their own coats of arms. I thought I'd join in with the Four of Pentacles (otherwise known as the [Four of Coins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_of_Coins)), which depicts someone who hoards money in the [Rider-Waite deck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rider%E2%80%93Waite_Tarot).

Here I depict the faerie crest on my own [coat of arms](/heraldry) guarding a coin, upon which is the quatrefoil from my shield and my motto **Omnia amittas quae non servantur** *"You might lose everything that is not saved."* There's something to be said about saving money there...

It's a little bit of a callback to [Shopping Sprite](/art/shopping-sprite), which I drew six years ago and which I think is really cute.
